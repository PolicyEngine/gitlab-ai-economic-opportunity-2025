# GitLab Foundation AI for Economic Opportunity Fund Guidelines

## Fund Overview

The GitLab Foundation AI for Economic Opportunity Fund is a partnership between GitLab Foundation and OpenAI that seeks bold projects using AI to expand economic mobility.

## Funding Details

- **Award Amount**: $250,000 seed funding per project
- **Total Fund Size**: Expected to exceed $10 million across all grantees
- **Duration**: 6-month cohort (March - September 2026)
- **Additional Support**:
  - 6 months of technical support from OpenAI engineers and other experts
  - OpenAI API credits
  - Access to peer learning network
  - Demo Day platform at OpenAI HQ
  - Eligibility for larger scaling capital through project partners

## Timeline

- **September 22, 2025**: Applications open
- **October 31, 2025**: Concept note deadline (5:00 PM PT)
- **November 14, 2025**: Finalists notified
- **December 12, 2025**: Full applications due (if invited)
- **December 2025 - January 2026**: Grantee selection
- **March 2026**: Cohort orientation begins
- **August/September 2026**: Demo Day at OpenAI HQ

## Eligibility

### Organization Type
- U.S.-based nonprofit organizations (501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor)
- Programs can operate in non-U.S. markets
- Partnerships between nonprofits and for-profit startups, consultants, and technology experts are encouraged
- For-profit social enterprises considered case-by-case with fiscal sponsor or nonprofit partner intermediary

### Project Requirements
- Must use AI to expand economic mobility
- Must demonstrate transformative potential (not incremental improvements)
- Must be feasible within 6-month cohort period
- Must show path to sustainability and scaling

## Focus Areas

The foundation invites proposals that show how AI can meaningfully transform the systems that shape economic opportunity, including:

### 1. Reimagining Service Delivery
- Using AI to improve how people access benefits, services, and resources
- Streamlining enrollment and eligibility processes
- Personalized guidance and support systems
- Navigation of complex systems

### 2. Unlocking New Forms of Personalized Education and Training
- AI-powered skills development and career pathways
- Adaptive learning and training programs
- Skills matching and job placement
- Career coaching and mentorship at scale

### 3. Improving Efficiency at Scale
- Automating repetitive tasks to free up staff for high-touch work
- Data-driven decision making and resource allocation
- Optimization of service delivery
- Cost reduction while maintaining quality

### 4. Creating Entirely New Ways to Connect People with Opportunity
- Novel approaches to income generation
- New models for skills development
- Innovative benefits access mechanisms
- Alternative pathways to economic participation

## Assessment Criteria (Inferred)

While not explicitly stated, applications are likely evaluated on:

### Innovation and Transformation
- How transformative is the AI application?
- Does it fundamentally change how people access opportunity?
- Is this a novel approach or incremental improvement?

### Impact on Economic Mobility
- Clear connection between AI capabilities and economic outcomes
- Measurable improvement in income, employment, skills, or benefits access
- Scale of potential impact (number of people reached)

### Feasibility and Implementation
- Realistic 6-month plan with clear milestones
- Capable team with relevant expertise
- Appropriate budget and resource allocation
- Risk mitigation strategies

### Strategic Use of AI
- Thoughtful integration of AI (not AI for AI's sake)
- Specific OpenAI tools/APIs identified
- Clear value proposition of AI vs. traditional approaches
- Understanding of AI limitations and risks

### Equity and Inclusion
- Attention to serving underserved communities
- Addressing systemic barriers
- Inclusive design and implementation
- Awareness of potential harms and mitigation strategies

### Sustainability and Scaling
- Path to continued impact beyond grant period
- Potential to scale to more people or replicate elsewhere
- Partnership and collaboration strategy
- Revenue or funding model

### Team Expertise
- Experience in economic mobility work
- Technical/AI expertise
- Track record of delivering projects
- Diversity of perspectives and lived experience

## What Makes a Strong Application

Based on fund priorities:

1. **Clear Problem**: Specific economic mobility challenge with demonstrated need
2. **Transformative Solution**: AI fundamentally changes the approach, not just automates existing process
3. **Direct Impact**: Clear causal chain from AI capability to economic outcome
4. **Feasible Plan**: Realistic timeline, appropriate resources, capable team
5. **Equity Focus**: Serves underserved communities, addresses systemic barriers
6. **Strategic AI Use**: Right tool for the job, awareness of limitations
7. **Scalability**: Potential to reach more people or replicate
8. **Strong Team**: Expertise in both AI/tech and economic mobility domain
