# Full Application

This directory contains the full application (second stage) for the GitLab Foundation AI for Economic Opportunity Fund.

**Note**: This stage only applies if selected as a finalist after the concept note review.

## Timeline

- **Invitation**: November 14, 2025 (if selected)
- **Deadline**: December 12, 2025
- **Decision**: December 2025 - January 2026

## Structure

The full application will likely require:

### 1. Project Narrative
Detailed description of the project including:
- Problem statement and context
- Goals and objectives
- Target population and reach
- Theory of change
- Innovation and uniqueness

### 2. Technical Approach
Comprehensive technical plan including:
- AI/ML methods and models
- OpenAI API/tool integration strategy
- Data requirements and sources
- Technology stack and architecture
- Development roadmap
- Quality assurance and testing
- Security and privacy considerations

### 3. Impact and Evaluation
Measurement and learning plan including:
- Success metrics and KPIs
- Evaluation methodology
- Data collection and analysis
- Baseline and targets
- Learning questions
- Reporting and dissemination

### 4. Implementation Plan
Work plan and timeline including:
- Phases and milestones
- Activities and deliverables
- Responsible parties
- Dependencies and sequencing
- Risk mitigation

### 5. Team and Capacity
Organization and team details including:
- Organizational background and mission
- Track record and relevant experience
- Team bios and qualifications
- Partnership and collaboration strategy
- Governance and decision-making
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion approach

### 6. Budget and Financial Information
Comprehensive budget including:
- Detailed line-item budget
- Budget narrative and justification
- Organizational financial health
- Other funding sources
- Sustainability plan

### 7. Sustainability and Scaling
Long-term vision including:
- Path beyond grant period
- Revenue or funding model
- Scaling strategy
- Replication potential
- Open source and knowledge sharing

### 8. Ethics and Equity
Responsible AI approach including:
- Ethical considerations
- Potential harms and mitigation
- Bias and fairness
- Transparency and explainability
- User consent and agency
- Accessibility

## Tips for Strong Full Applications

1. **Build on concept note**: Expand on the approved concept, don't pivot
2. **Be comprehensive**: Provide all requested details
3. **Show feasibility**: Demonstrate you can deliver
4. **Address risks**: Acknowledge challenges and mitigation strategies
5. **Quantify impact**: Provide specific, measurable outcomes
6. **Highlight expertise**: Showcase team capabilities
7. **Show strategic use of OpenAI support**: Explain how technical support and API credits will be leveraged
8. **Include evidence**: Use data, citations, and examples
9. **Make it readable**: Clear structure, headings, formatting
10. **Proofread**: No typos or errors

## Attachments

Likely required attachments:
- IRS determination letter (501(c)(3) status)
- Most recent audited financial statements
- Board list and bios
- Letters of support or commitment
- Work samples or prototypes
- Additional supporting documents

## Notes

If invited to submit a full application:
1. Attend any offered webinars or office hours
2. Ask questions about requirements
3. Get feedback on drafts if allowed
4. Start early - don't wait until the deadline
5. Have others review before submitting
